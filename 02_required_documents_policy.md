# Loan Application Required Documents Policy (Policy ID: POL-DOCS-2026)

## 1. Mandatory Core Documents (All Loan Types)
Every loan application must include verified references to the following mandatory documents:
1. **Identity Proof (`identity_proof`)**: Government-issued photo ID (Passport, National ID, Aadhaar, or Driver's License). Must be valid and unexpired.
2. **Tax / PAN Card (`tax_id`)**: Permanent Account Number (PAN) or national Tax Identification document.
3. **Address Proof (`address_proof`)**: Utility bill (<= 3 months old), Passport, or Rental Agreement.
4. **Bank Statements (`bank_statements`)**: Latest 6 months of primary bank account statements showing salary credits or business cash flow.

## 2. Employment & Income Documents
### Salaried Applicants
5. **Salary Slips (`salary_slips`)**: Latest 3 consecutive months of employer-issued payslips.
6. **Employment Verification (`employment_letter`)**: Employment offer/confirmation letter or corporate HR verification.
7. **Income Tax Document (`form16_or_itr`)**: Latest Form-16 or Income Tax Return (ITR) acknowledgment.

### Self-Employed / Business Applicants
5. **Income Tax Returns (`itr_2_years`)**: Last 2 years of filed Income Tax Returns with computation of income.
6. **Business Registration (`business_proof`)**: Certificate of incorporation, GST registration, or trade license (>= 2 years old).
7. **Financial Statements (`audited_financials`)**: Profit & Loss statement and Balance Sheet.

## 3. Loan-Type Specific Additional Documents
- **Home Loan**:
  - **Property Documents (`property_documents`)**: Sale agreement / title deed and approved building plan.
  - **Property Valuation Report (`valuation_report`)**: Bank-empanelled technical valuation.
- **Auto Loan**:
  - **Vehicle Proforma Invoice (`vehicle_invoice`)**: Authorized dealer quotation / proforma invoice.
- **Education Loan**:
  - **Admission Letter (`admission_letter`)**: Official university/institution admission offer and fee schedule.
  - **Co-Applicant Income Proof (`co_applicant_income`)**: Co-applicant salary slips or ITR.
- **Personal Loan**:
  - No additional collateral documents required beyond Core + Employment/Income documents.

## 4. Document Quality & Resubmission Rules
- Documents marked `missing`, `expired`, `unreadable`, or `unverified` fail the document completeness check.
- If any required document is missing or invalid, the application must pause and request corrected documents from the applicant/officer before eligibility evaluation.
